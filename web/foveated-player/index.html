FOVI:
A biologically-inspired foveated interface for deep vision models


Human vision samples the world non-uniformly — sharp at the center of gaze, progressively coarser toward the periphery. This leads to more space being devoted to representing central locations in the maps of visual space found in primary visual cortex (V1), a phenomenon known as cortical magnification.

FOVI makes this link explicit, linking a non-uniform retina-like sensor array to a uniformly dense V1-like sensor manifold. It allows us to plug foveated sensing into deep vision models such as CNNs and ViTs, enabling efficient high-resolution active sensing.
